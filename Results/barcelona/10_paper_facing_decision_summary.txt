ReliableVAEP PCS/RAR standalone validation
=================================================================

PCS/RAR is supported as a reliability-adjusted static ranking only if it is more reproducible than raw VAEP across independent non-overlapping match samples, especially in both outfield cohorts.

Primary 2015/16–2019/20 — outfield only
  n-points with higher median RAR reproducibility: 1/14
  pooled median Spearman gain (RAR - raw): 0.0000
  replicate fraction RAR better/equal/worse: 0.214 / 0.474 / 0.312

Primary 2015/16–2019/20 — all players
  n-points with higher median RAR reproducibility: 3/14
  pooled median Spearman gain (RAR - raw): 0.0000
  replicate fraction RAR better/equal/worse: 0.246 / 0.384 / 0.370

Validation 2008/09–2012/13 — outfield only
  n-points with higher median RAR reproducibility: 3/8

Validation 2008/09–2012/13 — all players
  n-points with higher median RAR reproducibility: 7/8
  pooled median Spearman gain (RAR - raw): 0.0000
  replicate fraction RAR better/equal/worse: 0.407 / 0.374 / 0.219

Interpretation:
- If RAR is consistently better in primary and validation outfield cohorts, describe it as a reliability-adjusted consensus ranking.
- If improvement is inconsistent or absent, do NOT call RAR a correction or a more accurate ranking. Keep it as a descriptive consensus summary.
- Original VAEP/90 and supported rank bands remain visible in all cases.
